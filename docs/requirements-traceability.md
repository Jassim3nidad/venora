## Status summary

This initial matrix intentionally does not claim full completion. The largest
release blockers are hosted Supabase/RLS evidence, the protected portions of
the E2E and accessibility suites, incomplete coordinator and dispute workflows,
live provider verification, and the failing
CI/deployment-verification gates on the current deployed commit. Local audit
remediation does not change production until separately authorized and pushed.

## Scope Decisions and Limitations

**Rate Limiting (SEC-03):** Venora’s current release is approved for free-tier infrastructure. Distributed multi-instance rate limiting is outside the current release scope. The application uses bounded best-effort throttling. Distributed enforcement is reserved for a future infrastructure upgrade when traffic, abuse levels, or business requirements justify the added cost.

**Future Upgrade Triggers:**
Distributed rate limiting should be reconsidered when any of these occur:

- Venora moves to a paid hosting tier.
- Traffic becomes materially larger.
- Automated abuse is observed.
- AI or payment endpoint abuse becomes costly.
- Compliance requirements demand distributed enforcement.
- Multiple production regions or instances require shared counters.
- The platform begins handling significant real customer volume.
